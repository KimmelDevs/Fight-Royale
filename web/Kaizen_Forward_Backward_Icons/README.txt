Kaizen Directional Bind Icons

kaizen-forward.webp = Z follow-up, moving Kaizen toward bound enemy.
kaizen-backward.webp = X follow-up, pulling bound enemy toward Kaizen.

Copy public/icons/skills/* into your web/public/icons/skills folder.
These are assets only; displaying them during a bind requires wiring the skill icon path in GameView or its HUD component.
